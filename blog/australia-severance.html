The NES Redundancy Pay

In Australia, the Fair Work Act 2009 sets the National Employment Standards (NES), which include the minimum redundancy pay for most employees.

Statutory Schedule

  • 1 to 2 Years: 4 weeks pay.
  • 2 to 3 Years: 6 weeks pay.
  • 3 to 4 Years: 7 weeks pay.
  • ... Up to 9 Years: 16 weeks pay (drops to 12 weeks for 10+ years due to long service leave overlap).

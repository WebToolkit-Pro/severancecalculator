Bahraini Labor Law Gratuity

In Bahrain, the Labour Law for the Private Sector (Law No. 36 of 2012) governs the end-of-service benefits for expatriate employees.

Statutory Formula

  • First 3 Years: 15 days of salary for each year.
  • Beyond 3 Years: 30 days of salary for each additional year.
  • Basis: Calculated on the last basic salary plus social allowance (if any).

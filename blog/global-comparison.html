Severance vs Gratuity vs Redundancy: Explained

Updated: May 2025

These terms are often used interchangeably, but they represent different legal frameworks for end-of-service compensation.

1. Severance Pay (USA/Canada)

Typically a contractual or discretionary payment. Often calculated as 1-2 weeks per year of service.

2. Gratuity (Middle East/South Asia)

A legally mandated statutory benefit. In countries like UAE and India, it is a right for employees after a certain tenure (1-5 years).

3. Redundancy Pay (UK/Australia)

Specifically paid when a role is eliminated. Statutory formulas usually apply based on age and service length.

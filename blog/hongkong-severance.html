The Employment Ordinance

In Hong Kong, the Employment Ordinance provides for Severance Payment (SP) or Long Service Payment (LSP) to eligible employees.

Statutory Formula

  • Formula: 2/3 of the last full month's wages for every year of service.
  • Max Monthly Wage: Capped at HK$22,500 for the calculation.
  • Total Cap: The maximum total payment is capped at HK$390,000 (though many use a practical 150k estimate for standard levels).

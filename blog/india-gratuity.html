India Gratuity Calculation & Rules 2025

Updated: May 2025

Gratuity is a statutory benefit in India paid to employees who have rendered continuous service for at least five years.

The Standard Formula

For employees covered under the Act, the formula is:

Gratuity = (15 × Last Drawn Salary × Tenure) / 26

Tax Implications

Under Section 10(10) of the Income Tax Act, gratuity is tax-exempt up to ₹20,00,000 (20 Lakhs) for private-sector employees.

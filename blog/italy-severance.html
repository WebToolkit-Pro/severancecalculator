What is TFR?

In Italy, the Trattamento di Fine Rapporto (TFR) is a portion of the employee's salary that is set aside each year and paid out upon termination, regardless of the reason.

Official Calculation

  • Formula: Annual Gross Salary divided by 13.5.
  • Accrual: This amount is accrued annually and adjusted based on inflation (fixed 1.5% + 75% of CPI).
  • Payout: Paid in a lump sum when the employment contract ends.

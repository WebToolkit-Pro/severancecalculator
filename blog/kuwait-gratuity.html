Kuwaiti Labor Law Gratuity

In Kuwait, the Private Sector Labour Law (Law No. 6 of 2010) governs the end-of-service indemnity for employees.

Statutory Formula

  • First 5 Years: 15 days of salary for each year.
  • Beyond 5 Years: 30 days of salary for each additional year.
  • Maximum Cap: The total indemnity should not exceed 1.5 years of salary.

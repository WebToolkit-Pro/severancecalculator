The Employment Act 1955

In Malaysia, the Employment (Termination and Lay-Off Benefits) Regulations 1980 mandate minimum payments for employees covered by the Employment Act.

Statutory Formula

  • Service < 2 Years: 10 days' wages for every year of service.
  • Service 2-5 Years: 15 days' wages for every year of service.
  • Service > 5 Years: 20 days' wages for every year of service.

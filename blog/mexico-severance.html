Mexican Federal Labor Law

In Mexico, the Ley Federal del Trabajo (LFT) provides strong protection for employees. Severance is mandatory for terminations without "just cause".

Official Formula

  • Base Pay: 3 months of Integrated Daily Salary (SDI).
  • Seniority Premium: 20 days of salary for every year of service.
  • Seniority Bonus: An additional 12 days per year (capped at 2x minimum wage).

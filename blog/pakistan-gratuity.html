Pakistan Gratuity Rules: What You Need to Know

Updated: May 2025

In Pakistan, gratuity is a mandatory end-of-service benefit governed by the West Pakistan Industrial and Commercial Employment (Standing Orders) Ordinance, 1968.

Eligibility

Permanent workers who have completed at least one year of continuous service are eligible for gratuity.

Calculation

The standard entitlement is 30 days' wages for every completed year of service, based on the last drawn basic salary.

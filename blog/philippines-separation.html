Philippine Separation Pay: Rights & Rules

Updated: May 2025

Under the Philippine Labor Code, separation pay is owed to employees terminated for authorized causes such as redundancy or retrenchment.

Authorized Causes

  • Redundancy: 1 month pay or 1 month for every year of service, whichever is higher.
  • Retrenchment: 1 month pay or 1/2 month for every year of service, whichever is higher.

The Saudi Labour Law Award

In Saudi Arabia, the End-of-service Award is mandatory for all workers. It is calculated based on your **last drawn total salary** (including basic and major allowances).

Calculation Multipliers

  • First 5 Years: Half a month's salary for each year of service.
  • After 5 Years: A full month's salary for each additional year.

Resignation vs Termination

If you resign, your award depends on your tenure:

  • Under 2 Years: No award.
  • 2 to 5 Years: One-third (1/3) of the award.
  • 5 to 10 Years: Two-thirds (2/3) of the award.
  • 10+ Years: Full award.

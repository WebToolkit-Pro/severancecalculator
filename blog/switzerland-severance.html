Swiss Severance Pay

In Switzerland, statutory severance pay (Abgangsentschädigung) is only legally required for employees over 50 with at least 20 years of service. However, contractual and industry-standard severance is common.

Industry Standards

  • Standard Formula: Approximately 1 month of salary for every year of service for professional levels.
  • Notice Period: Typically 1 to 3 months depending on tenure.
  • Unused Vacation: Must be paid out or taken during the notice period.

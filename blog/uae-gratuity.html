How UAE End-of-Service Gratuity Works in 2025

Updated: May 2025 | 6 Min Read

If you work in the UAE, the End-of-Service Gratuity (EOSG) is your most critical financial entitlement upon leaving a job. Governed by Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021, the system ensures workers are rewarded for their tenure.

Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for gratuity in the UAE private sector, you must have completed at least one year of continuous service. This applies to all expatriate workers.

The Calculation Formula

The calculation is based on your last basic salary (excluding allowances like housing or transport):

  • 1 to 5 Years: 21 working days' basic salary for each year.
  • More than 5 Years: 30 working days' basic salary for each year beyond the first five.

Impact of Resignation

Under the 2021 law, resignation impacts your gratuity differently than termination. If you resign before 5 years, your entitlement is reduced:

  • 1 to 3 years: 1/3 of the total gratuity.
  • 3 to 5 years: 2/3 of the total gratuity.
  • Over 5 years: Full gratuity.

What is UAE Gratuity?

In the UAE, end-of-service gratuity is a statutory financial benefit owed to employees who have completed at least one year of continuous service in the private sector.

Official Calculation Formula

  • First 5 Years: 21 working days' basic salary for each year of service.
  • Beyond 5 Years: 30 working days' basic salary for each additional year.

The total gratuity amount cannot exceed the equivalent of two years' basic salary.

Understanding UK Statutory Redundancy Pay

Updated: May 2025

In the UK, if you are made redundant, you are entitled to Statutory Redundancy Pay (SRP) if you've been with your employer for at least two years.

The 2025 Formula

The amount is based on your age and length of service:

  • Under 22: Half a week’s pay for each full year of service.
  • 22 to 40: One week’s pay for each full year of service.
  • 41 and older: One and a half week’s pay for each full year of service.

Important: Weekly pay is capped at £643 as of 2025, and the maximum number of years you can claim is 20.

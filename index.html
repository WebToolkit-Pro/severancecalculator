Frequently Asked Questions

What is Severance pay and who is entitled to it?
Severance pay is compensation given to an employee upon termination of employment. Eligibility depends on the reason for termination and length of service.
How is UAE gratuity calculated in 2026?
Under the 2026 UAE Labour Law, gratuity is calculated based on 21 days of basic salary for each year of the first 5 years, and 30 days for each additional year.
Is Severance pay taxable?
Taxation varies by country. In the UK, the first £30,000 is usually tax-free. In the USA, it is generally treated as regular income.

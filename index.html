How UAE End-of-Service Gratuity Works in 2025

Updated May 2025 · 5 min read · UAE Labour Law

If you work in the UAE, one of the most important financial entitlements you have is the End-of-Service Gratuity (EOSG). Whether you're an expat completing a contract or a long-term resident leaving after years of service, understanding exactly how this is calculated can mean the difference of thousands of dirhams.

What is End-of-Service Gratuity?

End-of-Service Gratuity is a lump-sum payment that UAE employers are legally required to pay employees upon termination of employment. It is governed by the Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 on the Regulation of Employment Relationships, which replaced the older Labour Law (Federal Law No. 8 of 1980).

Who is Eligible?

You are eligible if you have completed at least 1 year of continuous service. This applies to both UAE nationals and expatriates working in the private sector. Government employees and domestic workers may fall under different regulations.

The Calculation Formula (2025)

Service PeriodGratuity Rate
First 1–5 years21 working days' basic salary per year
Beyond 5 years30 calendar days' basic salary per year

Important: The calculation is based on basic salary only — housing allowance, transport allowance, and other benefits are excluded.

Resignation vs. Termination

  • Terminated by employer: Full gratuity entitlement
  • Resigned (1–3 years): 1/3 of total gratuity
  • Resigned (3–5 years): 2/3 of total gratuity
  • Resigned (5+ years): Full gratuity

When Must It Be Paid?

Under UAE law, gratuity must be paid within 14 days of the employment contract ending. Failure to pay can result in a complaint to MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation).

Gratuity Cap

The total gratuity amount must not exceed 2 years' total basic salary, regardless of how many years you've worked. This cap was introduced under the new 2021 law.

Pakistan Gratuity Law: What Every Employee Must Know

Updated May 2025 · 4 min read · Pakistan Labour Law

In Pakistan, gratuity is a mandatory end-of-service benefit that employers must pay eligible employees. It is one of the most important financial rights workers have, yet many employees are unaware of exactly how much they're owed or how to claim it.

Legal Basis

Pakistan gratuity entitlement is governed by the West Pakistan Industrial and Commercial Employment (Standing Orders) Ordinance, 1968. Additional provisions exist under the Industrial Relations Act 2012 and the Companies Profits (Workers' Participation) Act 1968.

Who is Eligible?

  • Permanent (confirmed) employees only
  • Must have completed at least 1 year of continuous service
  • Applies to both manual and non-manual workers
  • Does NOT typically apply to probationary or contract employees

How Gratuity is Calculated

ComponentDetail
Rate30 days' wages per completed year of service
BasisLast drawn basic/gross wages
Partial yearsNot counted (completed years only)

Example: If your monthly salary is Rs. 50,000 and you've worked for 7 years, your gratuity = Rs. 50,000 × 7 = Rs. 3,50,000.

Termination vs Resignation

Unlike UAE, in Pakistan gratuity is generally owed regardless of whether you were terminated or resigned — as long as you've completed 1 year. However, if dismissed for gross misconduct, gratuity may be withheld.

Provident Fund — Separate from Gratuity

In addition to gratuity, employers in Pakistan must contribute to the Employees' Old-Age Benefits Institution (EOBI). This is separate from gratuity and also payable at termination. Always request both from your employer.

How to Claim if Refused

If your employer refuses to pay, you can file a complaint with the Labour Court in your province or approach the Department of Labour. Keep records of your employment contract, salary slips, and any written communication about termination.

UK Statutory Redundancy Pay — Complete 2025 Guide

Updated May 2025 · 5 min read · UK Employment Law

If you've been made redundant in the UK, you may be entitled to Statutory Redundancy Pay (SRP) — a legal minimum payment based on your age, salary, and length of service. Here's everything you need to know for 2025.

Who Qualifies?

  • Must be an employee (not self-employed or contractor)
  • Must have been employed continuously for at least 2 years
  • Must have been genuinely made redundant (not dismissed for misconduct)

The 2025 Calculation Formula

Age During Each Year of ServiceEntitlement
Under 22½ week's pay per year
22 to 401 week's pay per year
41 or over1½ weeks' pay per year

2025 Cap: Weekly pay is capped at £643. Maximum service counted is 20 years. Maximum possible SRP = £19,290.

Statutory vs Contractual Redundancy Pay

Your employment contract may offer enhanced redundancy pay above the statutory minimum. Always check your contract. If your employer offers enhanced pay, they cannot pay less than the statutory amount.

Tax on Redundancy Pay

The first £30,000 of redundancy pay is tax-free. Any amount above £30,000 is taxable as income. This applies to both statutory and contractual redundancy pay.

What to Do if Your Employer Refuses

If your employer refuses to pay, you can claim through an Employment Tribunal. You have 6 months from the dismissal date to make a claim. If your employer is insolvent, you can claim from the National Insurance Fund through the Redundancy Payments Service.

India Gratuity Calculator — Payment of Gratuity Act 1972

Updated May 2025 · 4 min read · Indian Labour Law

Gratuity is a statutory benefit in India for employees who have completed at least 5 years of continuous service. Governed by the Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, it applies to companies with 10 or more employees.

The Formula

Gratuity = (15 × Last Drawn Monthly Salary × Years of Service) ÷ 26

"26" represents the number of working days in a month. "15" represents 15 working days per year of service.

Example Calculation

InputValue
Monthly Salary₹60,000
Years of Service8 years
Gratuity(15 × 60,000 × 8) ÷ 26 = ₹2,76,923

Tax Exemption

  • Government employees: Fully exempt from tax
  • Private sector employees: Exempt up to ₹20,00,000 under Section 10(10) of Income Tax Act
  • Amount above ₹20 lakhs is taxable as income

Severance Pay vs Gratuity vs Redundancy — What's the Difference?

Updated May 2025 · 3 min read · Global

These three terms are used interchangeably in casual conversation, but they have distinct legal meanings depending on the country. Understanding the difference is crucial when you're negotiating a termination package.

Severance Pay

Severance pay is a broad term for any compensation paid to an employee upon termination. It is most commonly used in the USA and Canada. In the USA, it is not mandated by federal law — it is a contractual benefit or company policy. The typical formula is 1–2 weeks of salary per year of service.

Gratuity

Gratuity is a legally mandated lump-sum payment common in South Asia and the Middle East (India, Pakistan, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Kuwait). It rewards long service and is calculated using specific statutory formulas. Minimum service periods (1–5 years) usually apply.

Redundancy Pay

Redundancy pay is the term used in the UK and Australia. It is specifically for situations where your job role is eliminated (not dismissal for cause). The UK has a statutory formula based on age and tenure.

Philippine Separation Pay: Your Rights Under the Labor Code

Updated May 2025 · 4 min read · Philippines Labor Law

In the Philippines, separation pay is the amount an employer must give an employee who is terminated for authorized causes. It is governed by the Labor Code of the Philippines (Articles 298–299).

When Are You Entitled to Separation Pay?

Cause of TerminationEntitlement
Redundancy1 month OR 1 month per year, whichever is higher
Retrenchment / Cost-cutting½ month per year of service, minimum 1 month
Company closure (not due to losses)1 month per year of service, minimum 1 month
Disease / Illness1 month per year of service, minimum 1 month
Just causes (misconduct, etc.)None
Voluntary resignationNone (unless in contract or CBA)

What Does "½ Month Salary" Mean?

In Philippine law, ½ month salary is not simply half your monthly pay. It includes: 15 days of pay + 1/12 of 13th month pay + cash equivalent of 5 days Service Incentive Leave (SIL). This totals approximately 22.5 days of pay.

Filing a Complaint

If your employer refuses to pay separation pay, file a complaint with the NLRC (National Labor Relations Commission) or the DOLE (Department of Labor and Employment). You have 3 years from the date of termination to file a money claim.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about severance pay, gratuity, and redundancy

What is severance pay and who is entitled to it?
Severance pay is compensation given to an employee when their employment ends — usually due to layoffs, redundancy, or company closure. Entitlement depends entirely on your country's laws and your employment contract. Countries like UAE, India, Pakistan, and Philippines legally mandate it; in the USA it's largely discretionary.
How is UAE end-of-service gratuity calculated in 2025?
Under Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021: First 5 years = 21 working days' basic salary per year. Beyond 5 years = 30 calendar days per year. Calculated on basic salary only (excluding allowances). If you resign within 5 years, entitlement is reduced proportionally.
What is the Pakistan gratuity formula?
Under the West Pakistan Employment Ordinance 1968: 30 days' wages for each completed year of continuous service. Minimum 1 year of service required. Only completed years count. Example: Rs. 60,000/month × 5 years = Rs. 3,00,000 gratuity.
How much is UK statutory redundancy pay in 2025?
Based on age and service: under 22 = ½ week/year, 22–40 = 1 week/year, 41+ = 1.5 weeks/year. Weekly pay capped at £643 in 2025. Maximum 20 years of service counted. Minimum 2 years employment required. First £30,000 is tax-free.
Is severance pay taxable?
It varies by country: USA — fully taxable as income. UK — first £30,000 tax-free. UAE and Saudi Arabia — generally tax-free. India — exempt up to ₹20 lakhs (Section 10(10)). Pakistan — typically taxable above exemption threshold. Philippines — generally exempt. Always verify with a tax professional in your country.
Do I get severance pay if I resign?
Generally no in most countries. However: UAE offers partial gratuity on resignation (1/3 after 1–3 years, 2/3 after 3–5 years, full after 5+ years). Pakistan typically still pays gratuity on resignation. Saudi Arabia: no award if resigned under 2 years. USA and UK: no statutory entitlement on resignation.
What is the difference between gratuity and severance pay?
Gratuity is a legally mandated benefit paid at the end of employment, common in South Asia and the Middle East (UAE, India, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia). Severance pay is a broader term — in the USA and Canada it usually refers to discretionary payments, often negotiated. Both compensate for service length but have different legal frameworks.
How accurate is this severance calculator?
This tool uses the official statutory formulas for each country, updated for 2025. It provides reliable estimates for standard employment situations. However, it cannot account for your specific employment contract, collective bargaining agreements, or unusual circumstances. Always verify with an HR professional or labor attorney for important decisions.
What is the minimum severance pay in the Philippines?
Under the Philippine Labor Code, the minimum separation pay for authorized causes is 1 month's salary — regardless of how few years you've worked. For retrenchment/redundancy: ½ month × years of service, with a minimum of 1 month. The formula includes 15 days pay + 1/12 of 13th month + 5 days SIL equivalent.
Can my employer refuse to pay severance/gratuity?
Where it's legally mandated (UAE, India, Pakistan, Philippines, Saudi Arabia, UK), employers cannot legally refuse. If they do, you can file a complaint with your country's labor authority. In the USA and Canada, if severance is promised in your contract, breach of contract applies. Always keep records of your employment dates, salary, and termination notice.

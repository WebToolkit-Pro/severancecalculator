Frequently Asked Questions

What is severance pay and who is entitled to it?
Severance pay is compensation given to an employee when their employment ends — usually due to layoffs, redundancy, or company closure. Entitlement depends on your country's laws and your contract.
How is UAE gratuity calculated in 2025?
Under Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021: First 5 years = 21 working days' basic salary per year. Beyond 5 years = 30 calendar days per year.
Is severance pay taxable?
It varies by country. In the UK, the first £30,000 is tax-free. In the USA, it is generally taxable as regular income. In India, it is exempt up to ₹20 lakhs.

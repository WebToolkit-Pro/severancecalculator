3 Severance Laws by Country
🇺🇸

United States

No federal law mandates severance. Standard practice is 1–2 weeks per year of service. WARN Act applies for mass layoffs (60-day notice or pay in lieu).

🇬🇧

United Kingdom

Statutory Redundancy Pay based on age + years of service. Half week's pay (under 22), 1 week's pay (22–40), 1.5 week's pay (41+). Capped at £643/week (2025).

🇦🇪

UAE

End-of-Service Gratuity: 21 days' basic salary per year for first 5 years. 30 days per year after 5 years. Based on basic salary only.

🇮🇳

India

Gratuity Act: 15 days' salary per year of service for companies with 10+ employees. Minimum 5 years of service required. Capped at ₹20 lakhs.

🇵🇰

Pakistan

Gratuity: 30 days' wages per completed year of service. Applies after 1 year. Also entitled to provident fund contributions from employer.

🇵🇭

Philippines

Separation Pay: ½ month salary per year of service (authorized causes). 1 month salary per year if due to closure/disease. Minimum 1 month's salary.

🇸🇦

Saudi Arabia

End-of-Service Award: ½ month salary per year for first 5 years. 1 month salary per year thereafter. Reduced if employee resigns (less than 10 years).

🇨🇦

Canada

Common law entitlement: 1 month per year of service (approx). ESA minimum: 1 week per year (max 8 weeks). Varies significantly by province.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is severance pay?

Severance pay is compensation given to an employee when their employment is terminated, usually due to layoffs, redundancy, or company closure. The amount depends on your country's labor laws, years of service, and employment contract terms.

Is severance pay taxable?

In most countries, severance pay is treated as taxable income. In the USA, it is subject to federal and state income tax plus Social Security. In the UK, the first £30,000 is tax-free. In UAE and Saudi Arabia, end-of-service gratuity is generally tax-free. Consult your local tax authority for specifics.

What is the difference between severance and gratuity?

Gratuity is a specific form of severance pay common in South Asia and the Middle East. It is a lump-sum payment calculated on years of service and basic salary, paid upon completion of a minimum service period (usually 5 years for gratuity, 1 year for general severance).

Do I get severance pay if I resign?

Generally no — severance is for involuntary terminations (layoffs, redundancy). However, in some countries like UAE and Pakistan, you may still be entitled to a partial gratuity even if you resign, depending on years of service. Philippines and Saudi Arabia also have specific rules for voluntary resignation.

How is UAE End-of-Service Gratuity calculated?

For the first 5 years: 21 days basic salary × number of years. After 5 years: 30 days basic salary × number of years beyond 5. The calculation is based on your last drawn basic salary, excluding allowances like housing and transport.

What is Pakistan's severance law?

Under the West Pakistan Industrial and Commercial Employment (Standing Orders) Ordinance 1968, workers are entitled to 30 days' wages for each completed year of service as gratuity. This applies to permanent employees who have completed at least one year of continuous service.

Can my employer refuse to pay severance?

If severance is mandated by law (as in UAE, India, Pakistan, Philippines), employers cannot legally refuse to pay. In countries where it's not legally required (USA, Canada partially), your employment contract terms govern entitlement. If denied unlawfully, you can file a complaint with your country's labor authority.

How accurate is this calculator?

This calculator uses the standard statutory formulas for each country and is updated for 2025 laws. It provides a reliable estimate but may not account for your specific employment contract, collective agreements, or special circumstances. Always verify with an HR professional or labor lawyer.

Statutory Basis under China's Labor Contract Law

Severance pay in the People's Republic of China is highly regulated. Under the Labor Contract Law (LCL), severance is generally payable upon the expiration or termination of employment contracts by the employer under statutory causes (Articles 40, 46, and 47).

Rounding Rules for Fractional Service Years

Severance is calculated based on the employee's length of service at the company (referred to as "N"):

  • Service of 6 months to 1 year: Counts as 1 full year (entitling the employee to 1 month's salary).
  • Service of less than 6 months: Counts as half a year (entitling the employee to 0.5 month's salary).
  • Service over 1 year: Standard progression applies. For example, 3 years and 7 months counts as 4 years (N=4); 3 years and 4 months counts as 3.5 years (N=3.5).

The N, N+1, and 2N Rules Explained

  1. Standard Severance (N): 1 month's salary per year of service.
  2. In Lieu of Notice (N+1): If terminating under Article 40 (illness, incompetence, or material change in circumstances) and the employer fails to provide a 30-day written notice, they must pay an additional 1 month's salary.
  3. Double Severance (2N): If the termination is deemed illegal (without legal grounds or process), the employer is legally obligated to pay double the standard severance (2N).

The Triple Average Salary Capping Limit

To prevent excessively large payouts, the law establishes regional caps:

  • The Rate Cap: If the employee's average monthly salary exceeds 3 times the local average monthly salary of the previous year (released by local municipal governments), the rate is capped at exactly 3 times the municipal average.
  • The Tenure Cap: For employees earning above the 3x limit, the maximum years of service counted towards severance is strictly capped at 12 years.

Step-by-Step Calculation Example

Let's calculate standard severance (N) for an employee in Shanghai with 5 years and 8 months of service, earning a monthly average of ¥25,000. Let's assume the Shanghai municipal average monthly salary is ¥12,000 (meaning the 3x cap threshold is ¥36,000).

  1. Determine Length of Service (N): 5 years and 8 months. Since 8 months is 6 months or more, it rounds up to 6 years (N = 6).
  2. Determine Rate of Pay: ¥25,000. This is below the Shanghai 3x cap limit (¥36,000), so the actual salary of ¥25,000 is used.
  3. Calculate Severance: 6 years × ¥25,000 = ¥150,000.

Taxation of Chinese Severance Pay

Under current Chinese individual income tax (IIT) laws, severance pay up to 3 times the local average annual salary of the previous year is fully exempt from tax. Only the portion exceeding 3 times the local annual average is subject to individual withholding tax, computed under favorable single-item tax relief structures.

Authentic FAQ

What is the N+1 rule in China?
The N+1 rule represents standard severance (N) plus 1 additional month of salary in lieu of a 30-day written advance notice under Article 40 of China's Labor Contract Law.
Is severance pay taxable in China?
Severance pay within 3 times the local average annual salary of the previous year is tax-free. Any amount exceeding that threshold is subject to individual income tax.
When is an employer required to pay 2N severance?
If an employer illegally terminates an employee without legal grounds or statutory cause, they must pay double severance, represented as 2N.

Our Commitment to Accuracy

At SeveranceCalculator.xyz, we understand that calculating end-of-service benefits, redundancy pay, and severance packages involves critical financial data. Because labor laws frequently change, relying on outdated formulas can lead to costly miscalculations.

Our Calculation Methodology is built on strict editorial guidelines to ensure high-accuracy, 2026-compliant results for all users globally.

How We Source Our Data

We do not rely on third-party aggregators or AI-generated legal advice. Our formulas are manually cross-referenced against primary legal sources from government labor ministries.

  • Statutory Formulas: We map direct legal statutes (e.g., UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021, UK Employment Rights Act 1996) into programmatic logic.
  • Capping & Thresholds: We actively track statutory caps, such as the UK's weekly pay cap for redundancy or the PRC's 3x average local salary multiplier limit.
  • Annual Audits: Our calculation logic undergoes rigorous review cycles at the beginning of each fiscal year to account for legislative updates and inflation adjustments.

Client-Side Privacy Architecture

Because financial calculators fall under YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) tools, privacy is our paramount concern. Our platform is engineered using a Zero-Knowledge Architecture.

All complex calculations are executed via optimized Vanilla JavaScript directly inside your browser. When you input your salary, tenure, or termination reason, that data never leaves your device. No backend database processes or stores your sensitive financial inputs.
